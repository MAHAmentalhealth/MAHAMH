FIELD GUIDE / 04 — FOLLOW

Policy & public action.

Track federal and state activity involving informed consent, psychiatric drug safety, patient choice, research reform, and access to non-pharmacological support. Use the underlying record to understand what is changing.

AREAS TO WATCH

What is changing and why?

Policy varies by state and changes over time. Use primary sources for the current text and status of a bill or rule.

Informed consent

Drug safety & reporting

Research transparency

CHECK THE PRIMARY SOURCE

Follow a policy from the record.

For federal bills, search Congress.gov by keyword or bill number. For federal agency rules, use Regulations.gov to read the text and comment deadlines. For suspected problems with medical products, FDA MedWatch explains how to report.

A SIMPLE WAY TO FOLLOW AN ISSUE
01

Find the text

Search an issue or bill number in Delilah or Congress.gov. Read the actual bill and note its jurisdiction and date.

02

Watch the status

Bookmark a bill for updates, then confirm the latest action on the relevant legislature’s official page.

03

Separate rules from bills

Agency rulemaking has its own record and comment deadlines. Use Regulations.gov for federal dockets.
