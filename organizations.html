CURATED DIRECTORY

Explore by area of work.

This directory focuses on work relevant to informed choice, psychiatric drug safety, withdrawal, peer and community support, research, and rights. Descriptions draw on each group’s public materials. Inclusion does not claim a formal partnership or ICI endorsement.

How to read this directory

Each entry identifies the work and why it is relevant to this guide. These are organizations and programs, not a clinician directory. External sites may change; consult their own pages for current activities and terms. Listings are not paid placement or blanket endorsements.

DIRECTORY SUBMISSIONS

Apply for a directory listing.

Organizations working on informed choice, psychiatric drug safety, withdrawal, peer and community support, research, or rights may request inclusion. Use ICI’s contact form and enter MAHA Mental Health — Directory listing as the subject. Include your organization’s name, website, contact person, a short description of its work, and why it belongs in this guide. We review submissions for relevance and accuracy. A listing is not a partnership, endorsement, or promise of funding.
