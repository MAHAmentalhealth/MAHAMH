A broader conversation

Better questions can open better possibilities.

Widespread distress has many possible contributors: social isolation, economic insecurity, trauma, physical health, environmental factors, and sometimes the treatments intended to help. Their relative roles are not fully understood.

No single profession, institution, or political movement has all the answers. We aim to bring scientific rigor, humility, and curiosity to the conversation while protecting access to care and the autonomy of people making decisions about it.

The Capacity Fund · Proposed

Help strengthen the field.

Organizations doing meaningful work often have limited funding or visibility. The proposed Capacity Fund would connect donors with vetted initiatives ready to use additional resources responsibly and effectively.

The fund’s host, governance, selection process, and donation pathway are still being established. This preview does not accept contributions.
