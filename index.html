01 / THE APPROACH

We need a wider view of mental health.

Social isolation, economic insecurity, environmental exposures, nutrition, technology, trauma, community disconnection, and the treatments intended to help may all play a role in mental health. We do not yet know the relative contribution of each. No single institution, profession, or movement has all the answers.

Progress asks for humility, curiosity, rigorous research, and a willingness to question long-standing assumptions. It also asks us to protect access to care and the ability of individuals, families, and clinicians to make informed decisions.

Our standard

We distinguish what is known, what is debated, and what remains uncertain. We link to the original source, describe its purpose, and revisit resources as evidence changes. Editorial inclusion is separate from financial support; no listing is paid placement or blanket endorsement.

A NOTE ON MEDICATION CHANGES

Considering a psychiatric drug change?

Withdrawal can be difficult and abrupt changes may be dangerous. Start with information about the drug, preparation, and support; discuss decisions with a qualified prescriber who can consider your circumstances.
