FIELD GUIDE / 02 — CONNECT

Support begins with connection.

Sometimes the most useful support comes from people who have faced similar questions and experiences. Explore peer connection, mutual support, peer respite, and community-based approaches.

FIND A PLACE TO BEGIN

Different kinds of peer support.

These options are distinct. Explore each organization’s approach, availability, costs, eligibility, and community guidelines before deciding what fits.

01

Online community

The Inner Compass Exchange is a peer-to-peer space where people share lived knowledge about psychiatric diagnoses, drugs, withdrawal, and life beyond conventional frameworks. Each person’s experience is their own.

Explore the Exchange↗
02

Peer respite

Peer respites are voluntary, short-term community settings. Programs vary by location, admission requirements, and the support they offer.

Find a peer respite↗
03

Hearing Voices groups

Hearing Voices USA lists groups where people can share experiences such as hearing voices and explore what those experiences mean to them.

04

Local networks

Peer-led organizations and community groups may offer connection close to home. Ask how facilitators are trained, how privacy is handled, and what crisis supports are available.

FINDING A FIT

Ask before you join or visit.

Peer support is not one uniform service. A few practical questions can help you choose a space that respects your needs and autonomy.

01

Access

Is it online or in person? Who can attend, when is it available, and is there a cost or waitlist?

02

Privacy & consent

What are the group’s privacy practices? How are decisions made, and what choices do participants retain?

03

Kind of support

Is this conversation, a facilitated group, a short respite stay, or something else? Ask what it offers and where its limits are.

Scope of these resources

The peer and community resources listed here are not intended for acute distress or immediate safety needs. Their availability and response times vary; check each program’s own information to understand the support it offers.
