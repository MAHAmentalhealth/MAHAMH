WHY CAPACITY MATTERS

Strengthen work that already exists.

Rather than creating another organization to duplicate the field, the Capacity Fund vision is to help credible groups deepen and sustain their work. Its focus includes informed choice, psychiatric drug safety, peer support, independent research, and policy education.

Selection standards, decision authority, reporting, and fund administration need to be set before contributions can be solicited for that specific fund. Being listed on this site does not entitle an organization to funding.

FOR FUTURE FUNDING

A credible fund needs clear safeguards.

Defined decisions

Review roles, approvals, and conflicts of interest should be established in writing.

Transparent selection

Funding decisions should follow documented criteria, separate from editorial inclusion.

Accountable reporting

Organizations should describe how funds were used, work completed, challenges, and outcomes.
