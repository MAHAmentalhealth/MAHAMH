WHY GIVE

Strengthen work that already exists.

Many organizations doing important work in mental health reform operate with limited funding or visibility. ICI brings together independent information, people with lived experience, and questions that deserve better research and public understanding.

ICI’s work includes education about psychiatric drugs and diagnoses, resources for people considering withdrawal, a peer community, and research and policy work on informed consent. Your gift is made to ICI through its dedicated MAHA Mental Health campaign.

THE LONGER-TERM GOAL

A fund guided by the people doing the work.

Our goal is a dedicated fund stewarded by a coalition of organizations, advocates, and campaigners who have worked on these issues for decades. We intend to publish clear review criteria and report how funds are allocated. Priority would go to organizations with strong track records aligned with the MAHA Mental Health reform agenda: informed choice, treatment safety, peer support, rigorous research, and accountability. The review group would retain discretion over awards under those criteria, including when circumstances change. Directory inclusion would remain separate from funding decisions.

This is the intended funding model. The donation link above currently directs gifts to ICI; a coalition award process and public allocation reports are not yet in place. The award process is not yet open.

FUNDING REQUESTS

Request consideration for your organization.
